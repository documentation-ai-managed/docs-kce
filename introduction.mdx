## Overview

KCE provides a centralized documentation space where you can organize and manage all your project documentation. You create structured spaces for teams to collaborate on technical docs, guides, and references. Use KCE to streamline knowledge sharing, reduce onboarding time, and keep information up-to-date across projects.

With intuitive tools, you build wikis, API references, and tutorials without technical barriers. KCE supports markdown editing, version history, and search, making it ideal for developers, product teams, and educators.

## Benefits for Teams and Projects

You benefit from faster knowledge access, reducing time spent hunting for information. Teams stay aligned with always-current docs, minimizing errors in handoffs. Project managers track documentation progress, while contributors focus on content creation.

## Quick Start

Follow these steps to set up your first KCE documentation space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account" icon="user-plus">

    Sign up at `https://kce.ac.in` with your email.

  </Step>

  <Step title="New Space" icon="plus">

    Click **New Space** and name it for your project, like `MyProjectDocs`.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add First Page" icon="file-plus">

````markdown
# Welcome to MyProjectDocs

This is your starting page.
````

    Paste content into the editor and publish.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Team" icon="mail">

    Share the space link and set permissions for collaborators.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<CodeGroup tabs="Markdown,HTML">
````markdown
# Project Overview

## Goals
- Deliver on time
- High quality
````

````html
<h1>Project Overview</h1>
<h2>Goals</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Deliver on time</li>
  <li>High quality</li>
</ul>
````
</CodeGroup>
