---
title: Cannot Invite Team Member
description: Help if team invite fails in KCE.
---

## Issue

- Invite email not received
- Error: "Cannot send invitation"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Email Address">
    Ensure the address is correct—no typos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend Invite">
    Use the "Resend" option next to pending invites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spam Folder">
    Ask your teammate to check their spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If issues persist, email support@kce.app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
